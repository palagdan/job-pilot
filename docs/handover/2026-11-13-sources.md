## Needs your sign-off

1. **`source.provider` + nullable `apify_actor_id`** on your table, and the CHECK constraint (written with explicit `is null`/`is not null`, per your gotcha).
2. The `"{{maxItems}}"` placeholder convention in `input_template`. The ingestion job fills it in, and also caps the Apify run with the same `maxItems`.
3. **`seedSources` now upserts** (overwrites by `key`) instead of your insert-if-missing. Your placeholder rows would otherwise never be replaced by the real actors. The seed list is the source of truth, including `enabled`, so change sources in `seed/sources.ts` and not by hand in the DB.

## Gotchas

- **Apify `startUrls` must be plain strings.** `{url}` objects silently produce 0 items and still charge the start fee.
- **Personal data in scraped items** (emails, phones, hosts, recruiters) must never be stored. The mappers drop them; see the list in `docs/sources.md`.
- `APIFY_TOKEN` is in Daniil's local `.env` only. The `apifyEnv` fragment comes with the ingestion task.
